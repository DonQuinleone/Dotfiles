Notification sounds bundled for notify-sound.

All five files are converted (ffmpeg, straight PCM re-encode, no
edits) from the freedesktop.org sound theme shipped in Fedora's
sound-theme-freedesktop package (/usr/share/sounds/freedesktop).
Converting to WAV avoids depending on an Ogg Vorbis decoder at
notification time.

    message.wav   from message.oga
    info.wav      from dialog-information.oga
    warning.wav   from dialog-warning.oga
    added.wav     from device-added.oga
    critical.wav  from bell.oga

Licence and authorship, per the theme's own CREDITS file:

        Ivica Bukvic, "Borealis" sound theme. CC-BY-SA.

    critical.wav
        Dr. Richard Boulanger et al., Berklee44 collection.
        CC-BY 3.0 Unported.

dialog-error.oga is byte-identical to dialog-warning.oga in the
upstream theme (same MD5 sum), so only one is offered here, as
"warning"; a separate "error" name would just be a duplicate of
the same sound under a different label.
